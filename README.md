# DocuHub Global

DocuHub Global is a fast, web-based platform providing free customizable templates, office formats, and business tools. 

## Features
* Tax Invoices & Employee Payslips
* Fuel & Maintenance Logs
* Rent Agreements & Legal Formats
* Live GST/Sales Tax Calculators
